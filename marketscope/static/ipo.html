PRE-MARKET INGESTION PROTOCOL

How new shares are added before 09:15 market open

Before Indian equities commence trading at 09:15 IST (pre-open call auction 09:00–09:08 IST), the Pre-Market Sync Engine polls exchange circulars, master security databases, and listing notifications at 08:30 IST.

Newly listed shares are automatically assigned ISINs, indexed into the active trading universe, and given instant chart and formula scoring hydration. Use the "Track Company" feature to keep notes, price targets, and observe initial trading sessions without losing context.
